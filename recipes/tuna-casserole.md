# Tuna Casserole

A baked tuna and noodle casserole with cheddar, Parmesan, and your choice of crunchy topping.

## Ingredients

- 2 Tbsp butter
- 2 Tbsp flour
- 2 cups whole milk
- 2 cups chicken broth or stock
- 8 ounces shredded cheddar cheese
- 1/4 cup grated Parmigiano Reggiano or Parmesan
- Salt, to taste
- Pepper, to taste
- Cayenne, to taste
- 4 cans tuna, drained
- 1 pound noodles, boiled for 5 minutes in well-salted water and drained

## Topping Options

Choose your preferred topping:

- Buttered and toasted bread crumbs
- Crushed potato chips
- Crushed crispy fried onions

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**.
2. Boil the noodles for **5 minutes** in well-salted water, then drain.
3. Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Stir in the flour and cook briefly, stirring, to form a roux.
4. Gradually whisk in the whole milk and chicken broth or stock. Cook, stirring, until the sauce thickens.
5. Reduce the heat and stir in the cheddar and Parmigiano Reggiano or Parmesan until melted. Season with salt, pepper, and cayenne to taste.
6. Combine the sauce, drained tuna, and noodles, then transfer to a baking dish. Add your choice of topping.
7. Cover and bake at **350°F for 30 minutes**.
8. Remove the cover and bake for **another 10 minutes**.
9. Let stand to cool before serving.
