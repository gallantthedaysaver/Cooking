# Tortellini Salad

![Tortellini Salad](../images/tortellini-salad.jpg)

A simple chilled tortellini salad with tomatoes, olives, onion, cheese, cured meats, and Italian dressing.

## Ingredients

- Tortellini
- Tomatoes
- Black olives
- Red onion
- String cheese, sliced
- Pepperoni
- Salami
- Bottled Italian dressing

## Instructions

1. Cook the tortellini according to the package directions. Drain and allow it to cool.
2. Cut the tomatoes, black olives, red onion, string cheese, pepperoni, and salami into bite-size pieces as needed.
3. Combine the cooled tortellini and prepared ingredients in a large bowl.
4. Add bottled Italian dressing to taste and toss until everything is evenly coated.
5. Chill before serving.
